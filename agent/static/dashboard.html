Metric definitions & evaluation policy
Correctness
Does this turn’s response meet the latest travel request—cities, direction, dates, duration, budget and constraints? Uses final-answer day checks and a Phoenix LLM rubric.
Groundedness
Are material factual claims supported by independent fixture evidence? Generic activity suggestions need no database record. No material factual claims → not applicable.
Topic relevance
Does the response stay within travel, or appropriately redirect an unrelated request? A wrong travel answer can still be on topic.
Completion diagnostic
Retained in trace details and saved experiments for continuity. It overlaps with correctness and is no longer a headline metric or a trigger for new automated escalations.

Pass rate = pass ÷ (pass + fail). Pending, unknown and not-applicable judgments stay visible but are excluded from that denominator. Phoenix runs custom travel rubrics; human calibration is pending.

Benchmark scenarios have synthetic inputs and references. All answers, evaluations, traces and delivery receipts come from actual executions. Benchmarks never enter the conversation warning window.
